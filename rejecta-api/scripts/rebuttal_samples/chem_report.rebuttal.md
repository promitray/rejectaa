# Point-by-Point Response to Reviewers
**Target Journal**: Chemical Communications

Dear Reviewers,

We thank the Reviewers for the time and care they gave our manuscript. Below we respond to each comment in turn and describe the revisions we propose to make.

## Response to Reviewer 1

### Comment R1C1 (Bounded Agreement with Counter-Proposal)
> *"I would desk reject this for insufficient novelty. Thermally triggered release from Diels-Alder adducts and nitroxide-based fluorescence quenching are both well known, and the combination is not clearly differentiated from existing work. This manuscript would be better suited to a specialized polymer journal."*

**Author Response:**
We appreciate the reviewer's insights regarding the novelty of our work. We understand that both thermally triggered release from Diels-Alder adducts and nitroxide-based fluorescence quenching are well-established. We propose to enhance the manuscript by clearly articulating the specific aspects of our approach that contribute to the field, and how they may inspire further research across disciplines.

**Action in Revised Manuscript:**
Propose to add a note on the scope of the present work in the Discussion.

---

### Comment R1C2 (Humble Agreement & Revision Commitment)
> *"The synthesis procedures are described without sufficient detail on the characterization of the final products, which raises concerns about reproducibility. SEC and DLS data for P2 and P3 should be reported."*

**Author Response:**
We thank the reviewer for highlighting the need for more detailed characterization of our final products. We agree that providing SEC and DLS data for P2 and P3 is essential for ensuring reproducibility. We commit to including this data in the revised manuscript.

**Action in Revised Manuscript:**
Propose to add SEC and DLS characterization data for P2 and P3 to the Results section.

---

## Response to Reviewer 2

We agree that such a comparison is essential to contextualize our findings within the broader field. We propose to include a comparative analysis in the revised manuscript, highlighting the similarities and differences between our system and existing systems.

**Action in Revised Manuscript:**
Propose to add a comparative analysis with existing photo- or thermo-responsive release systems to the Discussion section.
